# LEET Product Recommendations

## How to read this
This is not a catalogue. It is the firm's typical shortlist by use case.
The actual quote and final spec comparison happens through partner portals.
Each entry captures the role the product plays and why it's preferred.

---

## IUL (Indexed Universal Life)

Products:
* Singlife Legacy IUL
* Manulife Signature IUL

Notes:
* Suited for clients who care about legacy now, or possibly in the future
* Must have dependents
* Minimum >$100k USD in extra liquidity, investments, or cash before considering
* Or high surplus income
* Singlife vs Manulife: Singlife more expensive but potentially higher cash value; Manulife cheaper with slightly lower cash value
* Both are arguably good — advisor can quote and decide

---

## Index Savings

Products:
* FWD Ember Wealth
* Manulife Signature Index Income
* HSBC Indexed Flexi Income

Notes:
* Suited for clients with an existing large portfolio of equities or cash savings
* They want a lower-volatility long-term wealth accumulator
* Poor market conditions don't scare them
* Indicative threshold: equity holdings > $200,000, or cash holdings > $200,000
* Useful as a diversification option
* FWD and HSBC offer a volatility-controlled index — advisors should learn to explain the mechanic

## Single-premium ILP

Products:
* TM goWealth Secure
* TM goWealth Enrich
* FWD Invest I

Notes:
* For clients looking to invest a lump sum into a diversified portfolio
* TM goWealth Secure has a high-water-mark feature — death benefit pegged to the highest sum assured during the policy
* Ideal for older clients who want to combine retirement income with leaving a legacy for family

---

## Regular savings ILP

Products:
* FWD Invest First Summit
* FWD Invest First Horizon

Notes:
* Default recommendation for clients with no investments, or just starting on a small sum of regular savings
* Suited for $300–$1,000 monthly contributions
* Aim to help them grow regular savings to 25–30% of income
* Typical profile: low cash savings and low investment amount relative to people their age

---

## Short-pay ILP

Products:
* FWD Invest Flexi VII
* FWD Invest Flexi

Notes:
* Ideal for clients who began planning 5–10 years ago and have moved to a new life stage
* They may want to increase contributions but for a shorter period
* Positioned as an addition to an existing portfolio
* Premium options: 3 / 5 / 7 years; minimum holding period: 10 years
* Best fit: retirement planning with a shorter contribution window
* Also suits clients with high surplus — after renovation paid up, after a pay bump, or in bonus-heavy jobs — as a tool to average money into markets
* For the shorter 3-pay option: also works for clients with a lump sum and a long time horizon (default recommendation in that case)

---

## Capital guaranteed, high terminal bonus

Products:
* Manulife Signature Legacy Harvest

Notes:
* Similar role to Index Universal Income
* For clients with spare cash who want to diversify into a long-term wealth compounder
* Profile: equity feels too risky, cash feels too boring
* Stronger capital protection than Index Universal Income
* Trade-off: potentially less upside

---

## Annuities

Products:
* Singlife Flexi Life Income II

Notes:
* 3-year capital-guaranteed breakeven
* Regular income that beats a low-interest-rate environment
* Ideal for large capital seeking diversification and better-than-fixed-deposit rates

---

## Term life

Products:
* FWD Future Protect
* Singlife Elite Term
* HSBC Term Protector

Notes:
* For primarily younger clients protecting their key working years
* Primary purpose: income replacement
* Best fit: young working adults; young families not yet ready to think about legacy
* Average income, average net worth households
* Highly usable for portfolio optimisation or coverage replacement

---

## Whole life (regular premium)

Products:
* Singlife myWholeLife Plan

Notes:
* Seldom recommended on its own
* Best combined with legacy planning when client wants early-CI coverage
* Good tool for early-CI coverage when client cares about cash value and surrender value
* Typically suits clients with higher planning budgets

---

## Whole life (single premium)

Products:
* AIA Platinum Wealth Elite 2.0

Notes:
* The alternative kicker to IUL — uses ILP sub-funds instead of an index
* Suited for clients who are not ILP-sensitive
* Cheap premiums and high cash value
* No guaranteed floor, but attractive policy illustration
* Offer as a choice for less ILP-sensitive individuals
